## 🎯 What this repository contributes

Connect an objective evaluator to pulse-search algorithms, including GRAPE, dCRAB and direct-search strategies.

## 🧪 Suggested first exercise

Implement a simulated qubit objective and log every evaluation before adding uncertainty averaging.

This is a proposed starting task. Consult the upstream README, examples and supported dependencies for executable instructions.

## ✅ What to check

Check parameter bounds, evaluation cost, stochastic noise, stopping rules and platform-specific dependencies.

## 🔎 Scope and limitations

Experimental optimization between trials is different from continuous measurement feedback during evolution.

## What this repository contributes

Represent states, operators and time-dependent models, then propagate the dynamics before introducing an optimizer.

## Suggested first exercise

Validate a driven two-level system against a resonant analytic limit, then add a collapse operator.

This is a proposed starting task. Consult the upstream README, examples and supported dependencies for executable instructions.

## What to check

Check units, solver tolerances, normalization, density-matrix trace and Hilbert-space truncation.

## Scope and limitations

A dynamics solver supplies the physical evolution; it does not by itself choose an optimal pulse.
